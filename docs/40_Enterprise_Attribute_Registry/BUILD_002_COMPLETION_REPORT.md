## What was deliberately reused, not redesigned

- `eal_reference` grammar validation imports `ai.eal.models_pydantic.CANONICAL_PATH_PATTERN`
  directly.
- `datatype` reuses `ai.eal.models.DataType`'s exact 11-value `Literal`.
- The namespace-matches-path cross-field validator mirrors
  `ai.eal.models_pydantic.EALAttributeRecordModel._namespace_matches_path` logic exactly, applied
  to `eal_reference` instead of `canonical_path`.
- The JSON/YAML load pattern (`_read`/`_load`) mirrors `ai/eal/test_eal.py`'s helper.
- The doc set's style, structure, and self-check convention mirror
  `docs/20_Attribute_Language/` throughout.
