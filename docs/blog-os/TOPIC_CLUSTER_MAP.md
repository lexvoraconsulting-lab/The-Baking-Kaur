separately (`DESIGN_REVIEW.md` S-13).

A hamper pillar was drafted and **not** added. `cake-hampers` (119) and `luxury-diwali-hampers`

decision (`CLAUDE.md`). If hampers are published, this pillar activates — it is one of the four
re-open triggers in `COMPLETION_AUDIT.md`.

## Cannibalization check

Every pillar tested against the live collection and page set.

| Pillar | Nearest live URL | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| CG-P1 Custom & theme | `/collections/custom-cakes-meerut` | ✅ collection = "buy custom"; pillar = "how to brief one" |
| CG-P2 Sizing | *none* | ✅ white space |
| CG-P3 Flavours | *none* | ✅ white space |
| CG-P4 Cost & lead time | *none* | ✅ white space |
| CG-P5 Eggless | `/pages/100-percent-eggless-bakery` | ⚠️ **highest risk in the map.** Page owns the claim, pillar owns the explanation. Pillar links up; page is the hub |
| CG-P6 Keeping & craft | `/pages/freshness-guarantee` (draft) | ✅ once the page publishes as the hub |
| **CG-P7 Wedding & event** | `/collections/wedding-cakes`, `showstopper-wedding-cake` | ⚠️ clean only **after** Phase 0 reconciles the two wedding collections (`wedding-cakes` currently matches the substring "wed") |
| **CG-P8 Anniversary** | `/collections/anniversary-cakes` | ✅ collection = buy; pillar = choosing by milestone |
| ME-P1 Delivery | 8 live delivery URLs | ❌ **hard blocked** until Phase 0 consolidation |
| ME-P2 Celebrating | *none* | ✅ white space |
| ME-P3 Festive | `/collections/luxury-diwali-hampers` | ✅ collection = buy; pillar = when to order. Hamper *inventory* is empty — pillar drives to `/collections/cakes` |

**One pillar (ME-P1) hard-blocked on Phase 0; one (CG-P7) conditional on it.** v1 had three
hard-blocked, because v1 had three pillars sitting on the store's most duplicated intents.

**Four of eleven pillars sit in genuine white space** — sizing, flavour, cost, and celebrating in
Meerut. Fastest wins in the plan, nothing blocking them.
