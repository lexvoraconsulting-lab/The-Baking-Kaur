# SECTION 1 — Brand Strategy

## Brand Mission

To make 100% eggless celebration cakes the obvious, uncompromised choice in Meerut — so that no one
at the table has to sit out the cake because of what's in it.

*(Derived from the one consistently-repeated, verified differentiator across every independent page
checked this project: eggless-as-standard, not eggless-as-substitute. Formal sign-off on this exact
wording: `BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED`.)*

## Vision

To be Meerut's default name for custom celebration cakes and gifting — the studio people think of
first for a birthday, wedding, or milestone, without needing to ask if it's eggless.

*(`BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED` for formal adoption — synthesized from the real, repeated positioning
already in use, not an invented aspiration.)*

## Core Values

Derived from what the business's own real, live content consistently demonstrates (not invented
traits):

1. **Verifiability over persuasion** — this value is not aspirational, it is already this project's
   enforced engineering discipline (see `CLAUDE.md`): no rating, review, count, or certification ships
   without a real source. Thirteen fabricated trust claims have already been found and removed.
2. **Eggless without compromise** — not a dietary accommodation, the default recipe.
3. **Made-to-order freshness** — no advance stock, baked per order, repeated consistently.
4. **Craftsmanship in custom/designer work** — wedding, theme, and sculpted cakes are a stated
   specialization, not an afterthought.

*(`BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED` to formally ratify this list — it is inferred from consistent, real
content patterns, not a workshop-derived values statement.)*

## Brand Promise

"Every cake is 100% eggless, made fresh to order for your celebration." *(This exact sentence is
safe — every clause traces to a verified fact in `BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §2/§10.)* Do not extend this
promise with delivery-time guarantees, review counts, or satisfaction guarantees beyond what
`BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §9 (Policies) currently and consistently states — and note that §9's Refund/
Cancellation terms are **currently in live conflict (B1, unresolved)**; do not promise specific
refund/cancellation terms in any content until that resolves.

## USP (Unique Selling Proposition)

**Primary, verified**: 100% eggless as the studio's only recipe base — not a special-order variant —
combined with same-day, midnight, and express delivery in Meerut, and custom/designer/wedding cake
specialization.

**Do not claim** (per `BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §10, currently unverifiable): "highest-rated," "most
trusted," "X happy customers," "award-winning," any specific years-in-business figure. These
categories have already caused fabricated-content removals on this project three separate times.

## Positioning

Premium/luxury positioning (`priceRange: ₹₹` in schema; "Luxury Eggless Creations" tagline; "bespoke,"
"designer," "premium ingredients" language throughout About Us) within a single-location, Meerut-only
service area. Positioned against generic bakeries by specialization (100% eggless, custom design) and
against other eggless options by not treating eggless as a niche add-on.

## Target Audience

Verified, evidence-based inference only (real personas/demographics were never researched — flagged
below):

- Meerut-based households and individuals celebrating birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, and
  festivals, who want a cake or hamper that looks premium/custom rather than off-the-shelf.
- Households with dietary needs or preferences around eggs (vegetarian, religious, or personal
  preference) who currently must ask/verify at a typical bakery — this store removes that friction
  by design.
- Corporate/business gifting buyers within Meerut (per the real, live "Corporate Gifting Solutions"
  content on the Gift Hampers page).

**`BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED`**: no market research, customer survey, or demographic data exists
behind this — it is inferred from the product line and stated services, not measured.

## Customer Personas

**`BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED` in full** — no real persona research exists. The two sketches below
are directional placeholders derived only from the verified product/service lines in
`BUSINESS_MASTER.md`, not real customer data, and must not be treated as market-validated:

1. **"The Celebration Planner"** — plans a birthday/anniversary/wedding cake in advance, values
   custom design and eggless assurance, likely to browse collections before contacting via WhatsApp.
2. **"The Last-Minute Gifter"** — needs same-day, midnight, or 30-minute delivery, values speed and a
   working WhatsApp/phone contact over browsing depth.

## Tone of Voice

Derived from the actual, already-published copy voice across the FAQ, About Us, and delivery pages
(not invented — this is a description of the voice already in consistent use):

- **Warm but precise** — short declarative sentences, real specifics over vague superlatives (e.g.
  "baked fresh to order" rather than "the best cakes in town").
  - see: FAQ answer for "Are all your cakes eggless?" — "Yes. Every cake we bake is 100% eggless —
    it isn't a separate range or a special request, it's simply how we bake for every customer,
    every day."
- **Confident without inflating** — states real differentiators plainly, never leans on unverifiable
  claims to compensate.
- **Second person, direct** — "you," "your celebration," addressed conversationally, not corporate.
- **No exclamation-heavy marketing register** — the real copy uses restraint; avoid ALL-CAPS urgency
  or "Limited Time!" framing inconsistent with the premium positioning.

## Writing Style

- Sentence case for headings (matches real content: "Frequently asked questions," not "Frequently
  Asked Questions" in body copy, though page **titles** do use title case — follow whichever
  convention the specific surface already uses; don't introduce a third).
- Oxford comma: not consistently used in existing copy either way — default to omitting it to match
  the majority of real sampled copy, but this is a low-stakes style call, not a hard rule.
- Numbers: spell out one through nine in prose, numerals for 10+ and all measurements/prices/times
  (matches real usage: "5-7 business days," "30-Minute," "100% eggless").
- Currency: ₹ symbol, no space (₹350), matching real usage in `CLAUDE.md`/`BUSINESS_MASTER.md`.

## Messaging Rules

1. Never state a rating, review count, customer count, "years in business," or award unless it
   traces to a real, currently-verified source in `BUSINESS_MASTER.md`. This is the single
   most-violated-then-corrected rule on this project historically — treat it as absolute.
2. Never state specific refund/cancellation/shipping timing windows in new content until B1
   (`BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §9) resolves — the current live sources contradict each other.
3. Never state a specific delivery-area locality list in new content until B4 (`BUSINESS_MASTER.md`
   §7) resolves — the two existing lists disagree.
4. Never claim FSSAI/GST compliance with a specific number — the number doesn't exist yet
   (`BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §1).
5. Any new "years in business" or founder reference requires `BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED` sign-off
   first — none exists today.

## Spacing

4px base grid: `--tbk-sp-1` (4px) through `--tbk-sp-12` (48px) — see `tbk-tokens.liquid` for the full
9-step scale. **Rule**: always use a `--tbk-sp-*` token; never hardcode a pixel spacing value in new
component CSS.

## Border Radius

`--tbk-r-xs` (4px) through `--tbk-r-full` (100px, pill/circular). Buttons use `--tbk-r-full`
(pill-shaped) per `tbk-button.liquid`'s real implementation; cards use `--tbk-r-xl` (24px).

## Buttons

Real, shipped component: `snippets/tbk-button.liquid` + `.tbkx-btn` family in
`tbk-components.liquid`. Four variants, all already implemented:

- `primary` (`.tbkx-btn--primary`): pink fill (`--tbk-pink`), white text — the default call-to-action.
- `secondary` (`.tbkx-btn--secondary`): transparent fill, ink text, strong border — secondary actions.
- `ghost` (`.tbkx-btn--ghost`): tint-1 fill, pink text, no border — tertiary/low-emphasis actions.
- `link` (`.tbkx-btn--link`): no background/border, pink text, underline on hover — inline text
  actions.

Sizes: `sm` (40px), `md` (44px, the WCAG touch-target minimum), `lg` (48px). Always render new
buttons through `{% render 'tbk-button' %}` — never hand-roll a new button markup pattern.

## Cards

Real, shipped component: `.tbkx-card` family in `tbk-components.liquid`. Base card: white surface,
1px `--tbk-border`, `--tbk-r-xl` radius, `--tbk-shadow-sm`. Variants: `--soft` (ivory background),
`--accent` (gradient tint), `--hover` (lift + shadow-md on hover, respects
`prefers-reduced-motion`). New card-based UI should extend `.tbkx-card`, not introduce a parallel
card system.

## Icons

Inline SVG, stroke-based (not filled), `stroke-width: 1.9`, `stroke-linecap/linejoin: round` (per
`tbk-button.liquid`'s real icon markup). Sizes: `--tbk-icon-xs` (14px) through `--tbk-icon-lg` (22px).
**Rule**: new icons should match this stroke style, not mix in filled/solid icon sets.

# SECTION 4 — SEO Standards

## Title Templates

Products: `"{Name} - Eggless | Meerut"`, falling back to `"{Name} | Meerut"` past 60 characters
(real, already-migrated standard, `CLAUDE.md`). Pages: descriptive, keyword-relevant, matching the
real live pattern (e.g. "Cake Delivery in Meerut – Same-Day, Midnight & Express Service").

## Meta Templates

Type-specific hooked meta description per product (real, already-migrated pattern, `CLAUDE.md`); both
`seo.title` and `seo.description` must always be sent together in any Admin API mutation — the nested
`seo` object replaces wholesale, so omitting one nulls it (a real, already-documented operational
hazard, `docs/CODING_STANDARDS.md`).

## Heading Rules

One real, visible H1 per page. A known, unresolved low-priority defect exists (SEO-025: a hidden
`display:none` duplicate H1 sitewide in `layout/theme.liquid`) — do not introduce a second one
elsewhere while that's still open.

## Internal Linking

Link by genuine topical relevance, not template convenience (`INTERNAL_LINKING.md`'s stated
principle). Every new page should link to at least one topically-adjacent real page (FAQ, delivery,
eggless, or hamper cluster, whichever applies) — this project found near-zero cross-linking was the
default state before this pass, and fixed it deliberately, not incidentally.

## Collection Rules

Do not create a new collection that duplicates an existing one's product set under a different
keyword-targeted URL — `BUSINESS_MASTER.md` §11 already documents 7 such near-duplicates awaiting a
merchandising decision (B5, unresolved). Any new collection must have a genuinely distinct product
set or filtering logic.

## Product Naming

Follow the real, established human-readable pattern where possible (e.g.
`motu-patlu-designer-birthday-cake-meerut`); do not introduce new opaque numeric/short-code handles.
Existing opaque handles (`b110`, pure-numeric handles) are a known, deliberately-deferred cleanup
item (`CLAUDE.md`'s 8-step handle-optimization checklist) — do not fix them in isolation outside that
process.

## URL Rules

New pages: `/pages/{topic}-in-meerut` or `/pages/{topic}-meerut`, matching the real, already-
successful pattern (`URL_STRUCTURE.md` §3). New festival collections: `/collections/{festival}-
hampers`, matching the real `luxury-diwali-hampers` precedent. **Never rename an existing live
handle** without running the full 8-step handle-optimization checklist — this is a standing,
deliberate project policy, not a suggestion.

## Canonical Rules

Every page type uses Shopify's native `canonical_url` object (`layout/theme.liquid`, already verified
correct, dynamic per page type) — never hand-roll a custom canonical tag. Once B1/B5 resolve, the
non-canonical policy/collection surfaces should get canonical tags pointing at whichever becomes the
approved single source, per `POLICY_ARCHITECTURE.md`/`URL_STRUCTURE.md` §4.

# SECTION 6 — Development Standards

## Liquid

Reuse existing components before writing new markup: `{% render 'tbk-button' %}` for any button,
`.tbkx-card`/`.tbkx-*` classes from `tbk-components.liquid` for cards/layout primitives, `--tbk-*`
tokens from `tbk-tokens.liquid` for every color/spacing/radius/shadow value. Never hardcode a hex
color, pixel spacing, or shadow value that already has a token. `layout/theme.liquid`'s render order
matters — a real, already-encountered drift issue occurred when `tbk-tokens`/`tbk-components` render
calls existed in git but not live; always verify via a live pull before assuming a render call is
active.

## CSS

Mobile-first, 4px base grid (`--tbk-sp-*`), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected at the token level
(already implemented in `tbk-tokens.liquid` — extend this pattern, don't bypass it with unconditional
transitions). Component classes are prefixed `tbkx-` (real, existing convention) — follow it for any
new domain-agnostic primitive.

## JavaScript

No JavaScript-only interaction pattern where a native HTML equivalent exists — the FAQ/mobile-drawer
disclosure pattern (`<details>/<summary>`) is real, accessible, and already the established default;
don't replace it with a custom JS accordion.

## Accessibility

- Minimum touch target: 44px (`--tbk-touch`), 48px for large targets (`--tbk-touch-lg`) — real,
  already-defined WCAG 2.5.5 tokens; never ship an interactive element smaller than this.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already implemented at the token level).
- Native semantic elements (`<details>`, `<summary>`, real heading hierarchy) over ARIA-heavy custom
  widgets, matching the real, already-shipped pattern.
- `aria-label` required on icon-only buttons (already enforced in `tbk-button.liquid`'s own logic).

## Performance

- Never lazy-load the first/LCP image in a gallery (real, already-fixed defect, SEO-026).
- Reuse existing CSS custom properties instead of duplicating styles per component — reduces payload.
- Full Core Web Vitals measurement is currently blocked by the storefront's intentional password gate
  (`CLAUDE.md`) — do not claim a Lighthouse/PageSpeed score until the gate lifts and a real
  measurement exists.

## Theme Check

Run `shopify theme check --path .` after every theme-file change (the real, established practice this
project follows every pass). The current baseline is 1,369 pre-existing offenses across 94 files —
compare new output against this baseline; a new change should introduce **zero new** offenses in the
files it touches, not necessarily fix the pre-existing baseline (that's a separate, unscoped cleanup
effort).

## Documentation

Every theme-file or Admin-data change gets: a before-state verification (live pull/diff, or a live
Admin query), a stated reason, an after-state re-verification, and an entry in
`seo-audit/audit/CHANGELOG.md` plus `seo-audit/audit/AUDIT_LEDGER.md` — the real, established pattern
this entire project has followed for every fix so far. Do not skip the before/after verification step
even for a "small" change.

## Git

One logical change per commit; never bundle an unrelated fix into the same commit. Never use
`--amend` on an already-pushed or hook-passed commit (create a new commit instead, per this
project's standing convention). Never force-push, skip hooks, or bypass signing without explicit
user instruction.

## Commit Messages

Format already established across every commit this project has made: a `type(scope): summary`
subject line (`fix`, `feat`, `docs`), followed by a body explaining what changed, why, and how it was
verified — not just what. End with the `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`
trailer. Follow the existing `git log` style for this repo rather than a generic convention.

# SECTION 7 — QA Standards

## Pre-release Checklist

- [ ] Every new business-fact claim traces to `BUSINESS_MASTER.md` — no exceptions.
- [ ] No content touches an item marked 🔴 Conflicting or ⬜ Missing/Blocked in `BUSINESS_MASTER.md`
      without an explicit `BUSINESS APPROVAL REQUIRED` resolution first.
- [ ] Theme Check run, zero new offenses in touched files.
- [ ] Live pull/diff before and after any theme-file change.

## Theme QA

Pull the live copy of any file about to be edited, diff against git HEAD (`--strip-trailing-cr`,
since the CLI's line endings differ from git's) to confirm zero pre-existing drift, before editing —
the real, established practice every theme change on this project has followed.

## SEO QA

Confirm `seo.title` and `seo.description` are both set together on any product/page mutation (the
nested `seo` object replaces wholesale — omitting one nulls it, a real documented hazard). Confirm
canonical tags still resolve correctly for any new page type introduced.

## Accessibility QA

Confirm any new interactive element meets the 44px/48px touch-target tokens, has an accessible name
(visible text or `aria-label`), and that `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected if animation is added.

## Performance QA

Confirm the first/LCP image on any new or edited page/gallery is not lazy-loaded. Confirm no new
render-blocking asset is added without checking against the existing Core Web Vitals baseline
(SEO-026's fix).

## Schema QA

Run any new/edited structured data through Google's Rich Results Test. Confirm no `AggregateRating`/
`Review` is emitted without a real, verified source. Confirm both `Organization` and `Bakery` schema
entities still agree on `sameAs`, address, and geo values after any edit (the real, currently-open
SEO-020 gap is exactly the kind of drift this check catches).

## Launch QA

Before the storefront's password gate lifts: re-check `sitemap.xml` (currently 404, likely a gate
symptom, unconfirmed — SEO-019), and re-run a full Core Web Vitals/Lighthouse pass (currently
impossible while gated). Neither of these should be assumed resolved just because the gate lifts —
both need an explicit re-check.
