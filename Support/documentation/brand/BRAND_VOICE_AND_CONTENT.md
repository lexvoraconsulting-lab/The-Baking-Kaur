## 1. Brand Essence & Voice Pillars

The Baking Kaur communicates with warmth, culinary authority, and artisanal precision:
- **Warm Culinary Elegance:** Hospitable and passionate about flavor; never aloof or cold.
- **Uncompromising Purity:** Proudly 100% eggless; communicates ingredient integrity with transparency.
- **Artisanal Distinction:** Celebrates hand-piped artistry and tailored flavor profiles.

## 2. Copywriting Golden Rules

| S.No. | Principle | Good Copy Example | Forbidden Copy Pattern |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Verifiability** | "Handcrafted daily in Meerut with 100% eggless Belgian chocolate." | Fabricating fake review counts or "World's Best Cake" claims. |
| 2 | **Directness** | "Baked fresh for your celebration. Order by 2 PM for same-day delivery." | Vague promises like "Super fast delivery everywhere instantly." |
| 3 | **Artisanal Tone** | "Layered with house-made berry compote and light vanilla bean sponge." | Generic buzzwords like "Cheapest cakes in town with mega discounts." |
