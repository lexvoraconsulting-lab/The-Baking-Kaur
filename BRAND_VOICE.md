# BRAND_VOICE.md — The Baking Kaur

How the brand speaks. Governs all customer-facing copy. Mechanics in `COPY_GUIDELINES.md`; applied copy in `HOMEPAGE_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`.

## Personality
A Meerut cake atelier with quiet confidence. Warm, elegant, precise. The trusted artisan, not the loud marketer.

## Voice attributes
Premium · luxury · warm · trustworthy · minimal · celebratory · crafted.
Never: salesy · pushy · generic · keyword-stuffed · gimmicky · cutesy.

## Tone by context
- **Hero / editorial:** aspirational, spare, confident.
- **Product / merchandising:** descriptive, honest, desirable.
- **Delivery / support:** reassuring, clear, human.
- **Trust / craft:** proud but understated.
- **Error / empty:** kind, helpful, never blaming.

## Brand attributes (not categories)
**100% Eggless** is a brand attribute of the whole bakery — every product, every page. It is a trust signal, never a product category or filter (see CATALOG_ARCHITECTURE §10). Canonical explainer: `/pages/100-percent-eggless-bakery`.

## Pillars we always convey
Eggless (quiet strength) · handcrafted (real artistry) · fresh (baked to order) · local (Meerut pride) · reliable (same-day/midnight) · celebratory (the customer's moment is the hero).

## Do / Don't
- Do: "Freshly baked to order." · Don't: "FRESHEST cakes in town!!!"
- Do: "Delivered across Meerut, same-day and at midnight." · Don't: "Best cake delivery service Meerut online order now"
- Do: "100% eggless." · Don't: "Eggless (no egg) cakes for pure veg customers only"
- Do: let numerals prove ("20,000+", "4.8"). · Don't: adjectives without proof ("amazing", "world-class").

## The customer is the hero
Copy centers the customer's celebration, not the brand's greatness. We enable the moment; we don't upstage it.

## Signature phrases (reusable)
"Handcrafted for every celebration." · "Baked fresh after you order — never from a shelf." · "Delivered when it matters most." · "Dream it. We'll bake it." · "Gifting, beautifully done."
