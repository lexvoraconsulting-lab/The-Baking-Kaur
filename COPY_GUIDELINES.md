## Grammar & style
- Sentence case everywhere (headings, buttons, labels). No ALL CAPS except overline eyebrows (styled via letter-spacing, not typed caps).
- Oxford comma. Short sentences. One idea per line.
- No exclamation marks in body/CTA. Periods on full sentences; omit on short labels/CTAs.
- Numerals for stats and proof: "20,000+", "4.8", "12 AM". Spell out one–nine in prose otherwise.
- Currency: ₹ with no space ("₹1,500"). Indian digit grouping.
- Em dash — for editorial asides; en dash for ranges.

## Keywords (SEO/GEO)
- Each target keyword appears **once** per page, where a human would naturally say it. Never repeat for density.
- Primary local phrase ("cakes in Meerut" / "cake delivery in Meerut") once in H1/subhead; occasion terms in tiles/anchors; no stuffing.
- GEO lines = one clear factual sentence per topic (eggless, delivery, customization) an AI can quote verbatim.

## CTAs
- Verb-first, benefit-clear, 1–3 words: "Explore cakes", "Customise on WhatsApp", "Read reviews".
- One primary CTA per section; secondary is a text/ghost link.
- No "Click here", "Buy now", "Shop now!!!".

## Microcopy patterns
- Delivery: "Order by 4 PM for same-day delivery." / "Midnight surprises across Meerut."
- Trust: "100% Eggless", "FSSAI Approved", "Loved by 20,000+ customers".
- Reassurance near actions: availability, freshness, eggless.

## Anchors / internal links
Descriptive, keyword-aware, human: "Birthday cakes in Meerut" not "click here" / "birthday cake birthday cake Meerut".

## Accessibility of copy
- Link text meaningful out of context. Button labels describe the action. No color-only meaning. Alt text describes the image + natural keyword (no stuffing).

## Length guides
Headline ≤7 words · subhead ≤14 · supporting ≤22 · CTA ≤3 · microcopy ≤10. Mobile trims to the shortest legible form.
