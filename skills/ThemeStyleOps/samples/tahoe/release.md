## Release Readiness

A release is ready when every gate is proven, not when the date arrives.

| S.No. | Gate | State | Owner |
| ---:  | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | All `P1` plans implemented | ![Confirmed](../../assets/badges/tahoe/confirmed.svg) | Delivery lead |
| 2 | Verification checks passed | ![Blocked](../../assets/badges/tahoe/gap-blocked.svg) | QA |

| 4 | Rollback rehearsed | ![Planned](../../assets/badges/tahoe/planned.svg) | Platform |
| 5 | Client comms drafted | ![Draft](../../assets/badges/tahoe/draft.svg) | Product |

> [!NOTE]
> One blocked gate blocks the release. Ship the date, not the gates, and you ship the defect.
